# Original content policy v1

All Phase 6 questions, passages, answer options, explanations, tags, and
distractor rationales must be independently written original material.

- Real JLPT questions, commercial textbook material, unattributed bulk
  material, listening content, and copied translations are forbidden.
- Each item has exactly one correct answer and three reviewed distractors.
- The author attests `ORIGINAL_NO_COPY`; a different human reviewer approves
  every item against `review-rubric-v1`.
- `creationMethod` is `HUMAN`. Codex, CI, and automated generators cannot act
  as author or reviewer.
- Personal data, credentials, secrets, external source text, and contributor
  names or email addresses are forbidden in retained metadata.
- Exact duplicates fail. Every near-duplicate warning requires a distinct
  reviewer disposition.

This policy does not authorize production apply. Phase 7 emergency controls
and audit ownership must be accepted before production release is possible.
